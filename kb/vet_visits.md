# Vet visits

Healthy adult pets should see a vet at least once a year for a checkup and vaccine review. Puppies and kittens need a series of visits in their first few months for vaccines and deworming. Senior pets, usually from about age seven, often benefit from checkups every six months.

Plan extra time around a vet appointment. Arrive 10 to 15 minutes early, and avoid scheduling a long walk or grooming session right before, since stress and fatigue can make the visit harder.

Some procedures require fasting. For example, a pet may need to skip food for 8 to 12 hours before surgery or certain blood tests. Always ask the clinic what to do about the morning meal and medications before the visit.

After a procedure or vaccination, keep the day light. Rest and limited exercise are common instructions, and the vet will say how long to restrict activity.

Treat vet appointments as the highest priority on the schedule, and do not overlap them with other tasks.

Note: general guidance written for this project. Always follow your own vet's instructions.
