# Medication timing

Give medication at the same times every day. For a medicine prescribed twice a day, space the doses about 12 hours apart, such as 8:00 and 20:00. A medicine prescribed three times a day is spaced about 8 hours apart.

Some medicines must be given with food to avoid an upset stomach, and others on an empty stomach. Follow the label or the vet's instructions. If the instructions are unclear, ask the vet instead of guessing.

If a dose is missed, do not double the next dose. Contact the vet for advice, because the right answer depends on the drug.

Antibiotics must be given for the full prescribed course, even if the pet seems better. Stopping early can let the infection return.

Medication tasks should be treated as high priority and should not be dropped to fit a busy day. Never change a dose or schedule without a vet's approval.

Note: general guidance written for this project. It is not a substitute for veterinary advice.
