# Grooming and hygiene

Brushing frequency depends on coat type. Short-haired dogs and cats can be brushed about once a week. Long-haired or double-coated breeds, such as Golden Retrievers, often need brushing several times a week to prevent mats, and daily during heavy shedding.

Most dogs only need a bath every one to three months, unless they get dirty. Bathing too often can dry out the skin. Use a shampoo made for pets.

Trim nails about every three to four weeks. If you can hear nails clicking on the floor, they are probably too long.

Brush teeth several times a week if possible. Dental disease is common in pets and can affect their overall health.

Check ears weekly for redness, odor or discharge, which can be signs of infection. Schedule grooming when the pet is calm, and avoid doing it right after a meal or just before a vet visit.
