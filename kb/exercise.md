# Exercise and walks

Most adult dogs need at least 30 to 60 minutes of activity a day, split into two or more walks. High-energy breeds such as Border Collies, Beagles and Labrador Retrievers often need more, including time to run or play.

Small and toy breeds usually need shorter walks, around 20 to 30 minutes, plus indoor play.

Puppies and senior dogs should have shorter, gentler sessions. Puppies tire quickly and their joints are still developing, so avoid long runs and repeated jumping.

In hot weather, walk early in the morning or in the evening and bring water. Pavement can burn paws. Short-nosed breeds such as Bulldogs and Pugs overheat easily and should avoid strenuous exercise in heat.

Pets recovering from surgery or illness may be restricted from exercise. Follow the vet's instructions for rest.

Mental exercise such as puzzle feeders, training sessions and play counts too, and helps pets that cannot go on long walks.
