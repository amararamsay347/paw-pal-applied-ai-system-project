# Feeding schedules

Most adult dogs do well with two meals a day, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart, for example breakfast at 8:00 and dinner at 18:00. Puppies under six months usually need three or four smaller meals a day because they burn energy quickly and have small stomachs.

Adult cats are commonly fed two or more small meals a day. Cats that are fed once a day may vomit bile or eat too fast. Always keep fresh water available for every pet.

Avoid hard exercise for about one hour before and after a large meal. This is especially important for deep-chested dogs such as Great Danes and Labradors, which are at higher risk of bloat.

Keep feeding times consistent from day to day. Pets with diabetes need meals at fixed times that match their insulin schedule, so do not move those meals without asking a vet.

Note: general guidance written for this project. Confirm portions and timing with your veterinarian.
